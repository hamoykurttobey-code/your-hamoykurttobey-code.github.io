🇰🇷 Korea

Why I Want to Visit

Korea has captured my heart through K-Dramas, K-Pop, and Korean culture that I've come to love over the years. I've watched so many K-Dramas that I feel connected to the places, the food, and the lifestyle portrayed in them. I want to experience the vibrant energy of Seoul, walk the same streets as my favorite drama characters, and see the beautiful filming locations in person. Beyond the entertainment, I'm fascinated by Korea's balance between modernity and tradition, the warmth of its people, and the delicious food culture. Visiting Korea would be like stepping into the world I've admired from afar for so long.

What I Want to See and Experience

I want to visit Namsan Tower (N Seoul Tower) and see the love locks that couples leave behind, just like in the dramas. Exploring Myeongdong for shopping and street food, and walking through Bukchon Hanok Village to see traditional Korean houses would be amazing. I'd love to try authentic Korean BBQ, tteokbokki, and kimchi. Visiting K-Drama filming locations like the Pyeongchang-dong neighborhood or the bridges featured in shows like Crash Landing on You would be a dream. I also want to experience the Han River parks, visit Gyeongbokgung Palace, and maybe even attend a K-Pop concert. Korea represents a place where I can live out the moments I've only seen on screen.
