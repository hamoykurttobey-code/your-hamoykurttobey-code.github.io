🇶🇦 Doha, Qatar

Why I Want to Visit

My older sister works as a flight attendant for Qatar Airways and is currently based in Doha. I haven't seen her in person for a long time, and I miss her so much. Visiting Doha would give me the chance to finally reunite with her, spend quality time together, and see where she's been living and working. Beyond the personal reason, I'm also excited to experience the rich culture, stunning modern architecture, and warm hospitality that Qatar is known for. It would be a dream come true to explore this beautiful city with my sister as my guide.

What I Want to See and Experience

I want to visit The Pearl-Qatar, a luxurious man-made island with beautiful marinas and cafes. I'd love to explore Souq Waqif, a traditional marketplace full of spices, textiles, and local food. Seeing the Museum of Islamic Art would be incredible, as it's one of the most iconic landmarks in Doha. I also want to experience the desert dunes and maybe go on a desert safari. Most importantly, I want to spend time with my sister—catching up, sharing meals, and creating new memories together in this amazing city.
