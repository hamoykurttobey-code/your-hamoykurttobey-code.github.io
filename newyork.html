New York, USA

Why I Want to Visit

My grandmother lives in the United States, and it's been way too long since I last saw her. I miss her so much and think about her all the time. Visiting New York would give me the chance to finally be with her again, hear her stories, and make up for all the lost time. Family means everything to me, and reconnecting with her is something I've been dreaming about for years. Besides the personal reason, I'm also excited to experience the energy and culture of one of the world's most iconic cities—a place I've only seen in movies and always wanted to visit in person.

What I Want to See and Experience

I want to see the Statue of Liberty, a symbol of freedom and hope that I've always admired. Walking through Times Square and feeling the electric energy of the city would be unforgettable. I'd love to visit Central Park, especially in the fall when the leaves change colors. Exploring Brooklyn Bridge and the diverse neighborhoods of the city would be amazing. I also want to try authentic New York pizza and experience the subway system everyone talks about. But most importantly, I want to spend quality time with my grandma—sharing meals, catching up, and creating precious memories together.
