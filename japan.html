🇯🇵 Japan

Why I Want to Visit

Japan has always fascinated me with its perfect blend of ancient tradition and cutting-edge technology. I'm drawn to the country's rich culture, stunning natural beauty, and the respect and discipline deeply rooted in Japanese society. From anime and manga that I grew up watching, to the serene temples and beautiful cherry blossoms, Japan represents a dream destination where I can experience a completely different way of life. The food, the fashion, the history—everything about Japan feels magical and inspiring. I want to immerse myself in this culture and see firsthand what makes it so unique and captivating.

What I Want to See and Experience

I want to witness the breathtaking cherry blossoms (sakura) in full bloom during spring. Visiting Tokyo to experience the bustling streets of Shibuya, Harajuku, and Akihabara would be incredible. I'd love to explore Kyoto and visit ancient temples like Fushimi Inari Shrine with its thousands of red torii gates. Trying authentic ramen, sushi, and matcha desserts is a must. I also want to ride the Shinkansen (bullet train), visit Mount Fuji, and maybe even attend a traditional tea ceremony. Experiencing both the modern and traditional sides of Japan would be a dream come true.
